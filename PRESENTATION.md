# 📍 MarkPoint — AI-Powered Civic Action & Resolution Platform
*Pitch Deck — 7 Slides for Hackathon Presentation*

---

## Slide 1: 1️⃣ Problem Statement
### The Urban Civic Bottleneck & Accountability Crisis
- **High-Friction Reporting**: Complex municipal portals demand technical taxonomy and lengthy forms, discouraging 80%+ of citizens from filing reports.
- **Ghost Resolutions**: Municipal tickets are marked "Resolved" internally without physical on-ground repair work or proof of execution.
- **Zero Visual Evidence**: No verifiable audit trail or before/after visual documentation accessible to the local community.
- **Siloed Geo-Telemetry**: Lack of centralized, live geographic heatmaps for city administrators to prioritize high-risk disaster and hazard clusters.

---

## Slide 2: 2️⃣ Proposed Solution
### MarkPoint: Closed-Loop AI Grievance & Verification Lifecycle
- **📸 Zero-Form AI Snap & Report**: Citizens snap a photo. Multimodal AI automatically identifies the hazard, assigns the right municipal department, determines SLA priority, and drafts an official grievance memo.
- **🗺️ Real-Time GIS Heatmaps**: Live interactive maps with cluster pins showing active, in-progress, and resolved issues across cities.
- **👷 Govt Proof-of-Work Workflow**: Only verified municipal repair crews can upload timestamped after-repair photos upon completing physical field work.
- **🗳️ Citizen Consensus Voting**: Local citizens vote on before/after photos ("Verified Fixed" vs "Issue Still Persists"). Tickets only close once community consensus (>80%) is achieved.
- **🏆 Civic Karma & Gamification**: Rewards active citizens with karma points and trust badges for verified reporting and audit voting.

---

## Slide 3: 3️⃣ Target Users
### Tri-Partite Stakeholder Ecosystem
- **1. Active Citizens & RWAs**: 5-second visual reporting, live neighborhood hazard map, civic karma points.
- **2. Municipal Field Crews & Contractors (PWD, SWM, Water Board)**: Geo-tagged dispatch queue with exact visual context and digital proof-of-work for billing.
- **3. City Municipal Corporations & Ward Officers**: Real-time SLA adherence analytics, contractor performance audits, and automated escalation.

---

## Slide 4: 4️⃣ Technical Approach
### Modern Stack & Multimodal AI Pipeline
- **Multimodal AI Vision Engine**: Google Gemini Vision API for deep scene analysis + offline Edge rule classifier fallback for low-latency resilience.
- **Real-Time GIS & Telemetry**: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap with real-time cluster markers and multi-city syncing (Surat, Nadiad, Ahmedabad, Vadodara).
- **Cross-Platform Mobile**: Native Android wrapper via **Capacitor 8.5** with camera, offline caching, and native haptic feedback (4.8MB APK).
- **Continuous Delivery**: GitHub Actions automated workflow for continuous web deployment on GitHub Pages.

## Slide 6: 6️⃣ Scalability & Future Vision
### From Single Ward to Nationwide Scale
- **Dynamic City Federation**: Multi-tenant architecture allowing rapid onboarding of any municipal corporation with localized ward boundaries and SLA rules.
- **Proactive Fleet Camera AI**: Ingesting automated camera feeds from municipal buses and waste trucks to auto-detect potholes before citizens even report them.
- **Automated Escalation Matrix**: Rule-based escalation matrix auto-alerting Municipal Commissioners when critical SLA thresholds are violated.
